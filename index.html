Why Crater

Deep Texas roots. Eyes on space.

The Brown family has been in Texas since it was its own country, with long ties to the Rio Grande Valley. Crater LLC is run by brothers Preston and Hughes Brown.

Now rockets launch from Boca Chica, a short drive down the road from Brownsville, and the whole region is growing around it. We started Crater LLC in Brownsville to be part of that: we own and develop industrial land near the port and along the road to Starbase.
